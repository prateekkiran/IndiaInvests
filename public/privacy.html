India Investment Journey

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Private by design
India Investment Journey runs entirely in your web browser. Your financial data — statements, holdings, goals and plans — is processed and stored only on your own device. It is never transmitted to us or to any third party, because the app has no servers and makes no external data calls.

What we collect

Nothing. We operate no backend, no account system and no analytics that identify you. We do not collect your name, email, PAN, Aadhaar, bank details, portfolio values or usage behaviour.

Where your data lives

  • Everything you enter or import is saved locally in your browser's storage (IndexedDB) on the device you use.
  • If you enable the encrypted vault, your data is encrypted on your device using your browser's built-in cryptography (AES-GCM with a passphrase-derived key). Your passphrase is never stored and never leaves your device, and it cannot be recovered by anyone — including us — if you forget it.
  • Backups you export are plain files saved wherever you choose. An encrypted backup can only be opened with the passphrase you set.

Statement files and passwords

When you import a statement (for example a CAMS/KFintech CAS PDF), the file is read in your browser. Any PDF password you enter is used only in memory to open that file during import and is never saved, logged or transmitted.

No market data calls

The app does not fetch live prices or any market data automatically. Valuations come only from your imported statements and any prices you enter yourself.

Hosting

The application's static files (HTML, JavaScript, CSS) are served by Netlify. Like any web host, Netlify may process standard technical request logs (such as IP address and timestamp) to deliver the site and protect against abuse. These logs are handled under Netlify's privacy policy. We do not place analytics or advertising trackers in the app, and we receive no personal data from this hosting.

Cookies and tracking

The app sets no cookies and uses no advertising or analytics trackers. A service worker is used only to cache the app itself so it can work offline.

Your control

  • You can export your data to CSV or an encrypted backup at any time.
  • You can delete all data from your device at any time in Data centre → Delete everything, or by clearing your browser's site data.
  • Because data is stored only on your device, clearing your browser or losing the device means the data is gone unless you kept a backup.

Children

This tool is intended for adults managing their own finances and is not directed at children.

What this app does not do

It does not execute trades, collect money, initiate SIP mandates, file taxes, promise returns, or recommend specific products to buy or sell. It is an educational planning tool, and projections are illustrations based on assumptions you set.

Changes

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date above will change accordingly. Because we hold no contact details, material changes will be reflected here on this page.
